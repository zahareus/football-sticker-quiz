About TSV 1860 München

TSV 1860 München, nicknamed "die Löwen" (the Lions), is a Munich football club founded in 1860 — one of Germany's oldest. League champions in 1966 and DFB-Pokal winners in 1964, the club today plays in the 3. Liga with home matches at the historic Grünwalder Stadion.
